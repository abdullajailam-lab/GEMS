Making a lesson plan is now four short steps instead of one long form. The updated gems-v3.html is sent with two preview pictures, and its version line reads “16 Nov 12:05”.

  1. Plan: tap Daily or Weekly, pick the date, the teacher and the subject.
  2. Students: tick the students for this lesson. Students who are absent or not marked in attendance are greyed out.
  3. Indicators: each student has their own card showing only that subject’s indicators, and nothing starts ticked. You can tick indicators one by one, use Tick all or Clear for one student, or use Tick all for everyone or Clear everyone. “+ Add other indicators for everyone” adds subject indicators that aren’t in the students’ own lists.
  4. Finish: add the topic and duration. Quarter, week, assessment and resources are tucked under “More”. A summary shows how many indicators each student has, then tap Save lesson plan.

Back and Next stay at the bottom of the screen. Next won’t move on if no student or no indicator is ticked.

I walked through all four steps in a test and the plan saved correctly. A student with nothing ticked still gets saved in the plan, with 0 indicators. The summary shows that number in orange, so untick that student in step 2 if you don’t want them in the plan.

Fridays, Saturdays, and any holiday or break marked in the academic calendar are now frozen. The change is in the updated gems-v3.html, which I’ve sent; the version line reads “16 Nov 12:20”.

  • Attendance: on those days the screen shows a red “Attendance closed” banner with the reason. The P, L, A and M buttons, “Mark all present” and “Clear” stop working.
  • Attendance percentages: marks on those days aren’t counted in any student’s percentage, the attendance reports or the monthly summary. For example, a student marked present on Sunday and absent on Friday shows 100%.
  • Lesson plans: if the plan date falls on one of those days, the plan form shows a banner and won’t move past step 1 or save. A weekly plan’s start date must be a school day.

If you add a holiday to the calendar later, that day is frozen automatically.

Any marks already entered on those days stay in the app but aren’t counted. In my test I checked Friday 20 Nov, Saturday 21 Nov and the New Year holiday, and I tested blocking a mark and blocking a plan on a Saturday.
